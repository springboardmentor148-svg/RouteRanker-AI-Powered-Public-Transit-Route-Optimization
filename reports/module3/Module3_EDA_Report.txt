
ROUTERANKER
MODULE 3: EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS (EDA)
=========================================

1. OBJECTIVE
To explore the GTFS public transportation datasets and identify
patterns in scheduled trips, routes, stops, and service timings.

2. CITIES ANALYZED
- Bangalore
- Delhi
- Hyderabad
- Mumbai
- Pune

3. ANALYSIS PERFORMED
- City-wise route analysis
- Scheduled trip distribution
- Hourly trip patterns
- Time-period analysis
- Route-wise hourly comparison
- Top 10 busiest routes per city
- Top 10 stops by scheduled arrivals
- Headway analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Geographic stop mapping

4. OUTPUTS
- City route summary
- Route hourly summary
- Top 10 routes by city
- Top 10 stops by city
- Hourly arrival summary
- Route feature correlation
- City-wise correlations
- Final EDA summary

6. CONCLUSION
The EDA stage provides an overview of scheduled public transit
services across the five selected cities. It helps identify
differences in route structures, scheduled service frequency,
stop distribution, and timing patterns.

These findings provide a foundation for further data preprocessing,
feature analysis, and route optimization.

NOTE:
GTFS scheduled trips and arrivals do not directly measure passenger
ridership or actual vehicle operations. Further data is required
to establish actual route utilization or redundancy.
